# Wave 3 — International Index V2

## Outcome

Controlled technical rollout completed without an organic-performance claim. One future Champions League match, `barcelona-vs-feyenoord`, was localized in full for `pt-br`, `es`, `it`, `fr`, and `de`. This adds five indexable localized pages while retaining every untranslated fallback as `noindex`.

All five promotions belong to the Champions League. No Premier League, MLS, Libertadores, Sudamericana, other-league, or league-hub page was promoted in this controlled batch. Detailed league/locale inventories are retained in `baseline.json` and `after.json`.

## Integrity and index policy

The English source prediction remains unchanged: Barcelona -2.5 at published odds of 1.67. Fixture identity, date, time, factual limitations, numerical values, sources, and the absence of verified team news, lineups, and venue-split metrics are preserved in every localization. No prediction source or historical record was edited. Existing localized fallbacks remain available for navigation but excluded from indexing.

## Wave 4 recommendation

**GO only for another small technical batch; NO-GO for claims of organic uplift.** No usable GSC evidence was available. A future Wave 4 should remain limited to future fixtures in the priority competitions, require the same full-localization and noindex-fallback gates, and measure Search Console outcomes before any broad rollout.
